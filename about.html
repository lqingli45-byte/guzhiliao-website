About

An independent digital product brand.

Gu Zhi Liao is an independent digital product brand focused on AI productivity tools, knowledge management, digital templates, and practical work workflows.

The brand

Gu Zhi Liao was created by a founder in China who uses AI tools in daily work, including research, writing, document work, meeting notes, and a personal knowledge library. The aim is to turn that practical experience into clear tools other people can use.

What we focus on

The current product directions are knowledge management, work organization, content creation workflows, and personal review templates. These areas may be refined or expanded over time. Work still in development is marked Planned or Coming Soon.

The name

The English name is Gu Zhi Liao. The Chinese name is 谷知了. The idea behind the name is simple: make AI useful in everyday work. Gu Zhi Liao is a personal brand name.
